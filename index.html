      New game

      × mines Changing the difficulty starts a new game at once.

      Assists

      Each one counts as a hint: the win will not be a best time. Not available in puzzle or hidden-counter mode.

      The flag check works once per game and adds 15 seconds. Hint also shows every cell's mine chance when nothing is certain.

      Settings

      Mode, board, no-guess and power-ups are chosen in New game….

      Click + add key, then press the key you want. Esc cancels.

      Short beeps for reveals, flags, undo, wins and losses.

      The theme is chosen in the header. Animations switch off automatically if your device asks for reduced motion.

      Your game in progress is saved automatically and resumes when you reopen the page.

      Keyboard shortcuts and help

      Change keys in Settings → Controls.

      Mouse and touch

      • Click a cell to open it. Click an open number whose flags are all placed to open its neighbours (chording).
      • Right-click, or long-press on a phone, flags a cell; again for a ? mark. Flag mode makes plain taps flag.
      • Hover an open number to outline its neighbours and see how many flags it still needs.
      • The arrow keys move around the board; screen readers announce each cell.
